## Lead story — Iran strikes Kuwait & Bahrain

- Iran launched drones at Kuwait's Ali Al Salem Air Base (hosts US
  forces) and fresh attacks on Bahrain, after US aircraft struck an
  Iranian missile launch site on Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz.
- Kuwait's air defences engaged the drones; Bahrain intercepted several
  attacks before dawn. No damage or casualties reported by Kuwait,
  Bahrain or US Central Command. Iran claims it hit air defences at
  Ali Al Salem — unconfirmed, and the image says so.
- First Iranian attack on Kuwait since September; third attack on
  Bahrain in five days.
